# All, Tracked and App detail: device markers

Status: resolved
Spec: ../spec.md · Plan: ../plan.md (phase 3)
Blocked by: 06
Design: design/mac/All.dc.html, design/mac/AddTracked.dc.html, design/mac/AppDetail.dc.html (AppDetail still has the older header)

All: a small device marker (circle or square in the device colour) after each app name for the devices it ran on; in This scope, apps that exist only on another device are hidden. Add app › Tracked: device markers and the merged-count badge on the tile for apps on more than one device. App detail: a device split bar under the lifetime tiles; the year grid sums all devices in All scope. Add app › Running and Installed stay local to this device.

Done when: the three screens match the canvas boards "All", "Add app · Tracked" and "App detail" in All scope and are unchanged with one device.
